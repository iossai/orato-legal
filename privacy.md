# Orato Privacy Policy

**Effective:** 2 October 2026 · Applies to Orato on iPhone and Android.

Orato is a speech coach that measures how you speak — pace, clarity, pauses, pitch — and builds a training plan from it. It does all of this **on your phone**. This policy explains what that means in practice.

## The short version

- Orato has no account and no sign-in.
- Orato has **no network code**. On Android it does not even request permission to use the internet.
- Nothing you record, say or score is sent to us or to anyone else. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

## What Orato processes, and where

Everything below is created, stored and processed on your device only.

| What | Why | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Microphone audio | Measured while you record a session. Recording happens only when you start it. | Saved as audio files on your phone so you can listen back. |
| Transcripts of what you said | Counting filler words and checking how clearly you were heard. | On your phone, inside Orato's own storage. |
| Measurements and scores | Your six-axis profile, archetype and progress over time. | On your phone. |
| Your training plan | The twelve-week plan built from your first reading. | On your phone. |
| First language and speaking goal | Choosing which sounds to drill and which prompts to ask. | On your phone. |

## Speech recognition

Transcription uses your phone's **on-device** speech recognizer — Apple's on iPhone, the system recognizer on Android. Orato requires on-device recognition; if your phone cannot transcribe on-device, Orato turns off the features that need a transcript and says so, rather than sending your speech to a server.

On iPhone, the permission prompt for speech recognition is written by Apple and covers every app, so it may mention sending audio to Apple. Orato asks for on-device recognition only.

## Backups and moving to a new phone

- **Recordings** are excluded from iCloud backup on iPhone, and from Google backup and phone-to-phone transfer on Android. Audio does not leave the device you recorded it on.
- **Everything else** (plan, scores, transcripts) follows your phone's normal behaviour: on iPhone it is part of your device backup if you use one; on Android it is not cloud-backed-up but does move with you when you transfer to a new phone.

## Deleting your data

- **Settings → Delete all recordings** removes every audio file immediately. Your scores and transcripts stay, because they contain no audio.
- **Uninstalling Orato** removes everything it stored.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

## The research corpus setting

Settings has a switch to contribute recordings to research that improves Orato's models. It is **off by default**. In this version it records your preference and nothing else — no recording is contributed. If contribution is ever built, you will be asked again, with what is sent, how long it is kept and how to withdraw spelled out before anything happens.

## Purchases

This beta charges nothing and contains no payment code. When subscriptions arrive they will be handled by the App Store or Google Play; Orato will never see your payment details.

## Children

Orato is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for use by them.

## Not a medical service

Orato is a coaching tool. It is not speech therapy and does not diagnose anything; the measurements it shows describe delivery, not health.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it, and anything that alters what leaves your phone will be described in the app before it takes effect.
